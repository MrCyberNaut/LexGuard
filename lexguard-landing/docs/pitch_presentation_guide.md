# 📊 LexGuard Pitch Presentation Guide (Canva Slide-by-Slide)

This guide provides a slide-by-slide blueprint for building a high-impact, professional pitch deck in Canva for the **Google × Scaler PromptWars 2026** hackathon. It covers exactly **what to show on the slide** (visuals/elements) and **what to speak** (narrative script/talking points) for a winning pitch.

---

## 🎨 Slide 1: The Title (The Hook)
*   **Slide Header:** `LexGuard`
*   **Slide Sub-header:** `AI-Powered Legal Contract Intelligence`
*   **Visual Style (What to Show in Canva):** 
    *   Bespoke, premium dark background (deep void charcoal/black).
    *   A glowing neon purple/indigo abstract shield or digital blueprint grid.
    *   Clean, high-impact Sans-Serif font (e.g., *Space Grotesk* or *Outfit*).
    *   Tagline at the bottom: `"Every contract is written by their lawyer. This is yours."`
*   **What to Speak:**
    > *"Good evening judges. Let me ask you a question: Have you ever scrolled straight to the bottom of a terms-of-service, NDA, or employment agreement, clicked 'Accept', and hoped for the best? We all have. Why? Because hiring a lawyer costs $300 an hour, but signing blind is a legal ticking time bomb. Every contract you sign is written by their lawyers to protect their interests. Tonight, we introduce LexGuard—the 24/7 multi-agent AI legal advocate that stands in your corner."*

## 🛑 Slide 2: The Core Problem (The Unequal Playing Field)
*   **Slide Header:** `The Asymmetric Legal Landscape`
*   **Visual Style (What to Show in Canva):**
    *   Split-screen visual or two contrasting columns:
        *   **Left Column (The Corporations):** High-priced legal teams, ironclad protections, 50-page complex agreements.
        *   **Right Column (The Individuals/SMEs):** 0 legal budget, high litigation risk, complete lack of pushback power.
    *   A bold stat text: `91%` — *of users accept legal terms without reading them (Deloitte).*
*   **What to Speak:**
    > *"Here is the hard truth: legal agreements are intentionally designed as asymmetric warfare. Corporations hire massive legal teams to draft one-sided terms, while individuals and small businesses sign away their rights, intellectual property, and liability limits simply because they can't afford legal counsel. This creates a massive gap in trust and safety. We are here to bridge that gap."*

## 💡 Slide 3: The Solution (LexGuard)
*   **Slide Header:** `LexGuard: Your Legal Shield`
*   **Visual Style (What to Show in Canva):**
    *   A clean interface mockup showing a document upload panel and an immediate transition into a sleek risk-index bar chart.
    *   Key Pillars (as 3 modern icons with brief captions):
        *   **1. Parse:** Deep, structural document analysis.
        *   **2. Audit:** Precision risk classification.

*   **What to Speak:**

By uploading a contract, our specialized multi-agent AI pipeline tears the document apart, highlights hidden traps, ranks clauses by risk severity, and equips you with the exact language needed to negotiate back on equal footing."*

## 🧠 Slide 4: The Core Intelligence (The 3-Agent Gemini Chain)
*   **Slide Header:** `Behind the Scenes: Multi-Agent AI Chain`
*   **Visual Style (What to Show in Canva):**
    *   A horizontal flowchart showing 3 circular nodes connected by glowing lines:
        *   **Node 1: Parser (Agent 1):** Digests PDFs and outputs structured JSON clauses.
        *   **Node 2: Severity Assessor (Agent 2):** Evaluates risk levels (High, Medium, Low) and outlines legal reasoning.
        *   **Node 3: Defender / Advocate (Agent 3):** Generates role-aware pushback scripts and redlines for High-Risk items.
    *   A badge indicating: `Powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash`
*   **What to Speak:**
    > *"Instead of relying on a single generic AI prompt—which often misses subtle details—we architected a surgical, serial multi-agent pipeline powered by Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash. 
    > First, our Parser extracts the structural text. 
    > Second, our Assessor scores every single clause. 
    > Third, our Advocate dynamically activates for High-Risk clauses. It analyzes who you are—an employee, founder, or contractor—and formulates custom negotiation arguments tailored exactly to your role. It’s like having a team of specialized paralegals reviewing your document in parallel."*
