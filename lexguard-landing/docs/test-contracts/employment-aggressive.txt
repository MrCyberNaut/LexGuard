EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the date of signing between TechCorp Inc. ("Company") and the undersigned employee ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
Employee agrees to perform all duties assigned by the Company, including duties outside the scope of the stated job description, at the Company's sole discretion. The Company reserves the right to modify, expand, or reassign Employee's responsibilities at any time without notice or additional compensation.

2. COMPENSATION
Employee will receive a base salary as specified in the offer letter. The Company reserves the right to modify compensation, including reductions, upon 7 days written notice to Employee. Bonus payments, if any, are entirely at the Company's discretion and do not constitute a contractual obligation.

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT
Employee agrees that any and all inventions, discoveries, improvements, works of authorship, software, algorithms, business methods, and ideas — whether or not patentable — that Employee conceives, develops, reduces to practice, or contributes to during the term of employment, or within 2 years after termination, shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company. This includes work created on Employee's personal time using personal equipment if it relates in any way to the Company's business, current or contemplated products, or research. Employee hereby irrevocably assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work to the Company.

4. NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT
Employee agrees to keep all Company information strictly confidential, including information that is or becomes publicly available if it was disclosed to Employee in a confidential context. This obligation survives termination of employment indefinitely and without limitation.

5. NON-COMPETE COVENANT
During employment and for a period of 36 months following termination for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in, own, manage, operate, control, consult for, or provide services to any business that competes with the Company in any market where the Company operates or plans to operate globally. Employee acknowledges that the Company operates in a global market and agrees that this restriction applies worldwide.

6. NON-SOLICITATION
For a period of 24 months following termination, Employee shall not solicit, recruit, or hire any current or former Company employee, contractor, or consultant. Employee shall also not solicit any Company customer, client, or prospective customer with whom Employee had contact during the last 3 years of employment.

7. MANDATORY ARBITRATION
Any and all disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement, Employee's employment, or termination of employment — including claims of discrimination, harassment, wrongful termination, and wage violations — shall be resolved exclusively through binding individual arbitration administered by JAMS under its Streamlined Arbitration Rules. Employee waives the right to a jury trial and the right to bring or participate in any class action, collective action, or representative action. Arbitration shall be conducted in the Company's principal place of business.

8. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT
Employee's employment is at-will. Either party may terminate employment at any time, for any reason or no reason, with or without cause or notice. The Company may terminate Employee immediately without severance.

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND MODIFICATION
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. The Company reserves the right to modify any term of this Agreement upon 14 days notice to Employee. Continued employment after notice constitutes acceptance of the modified terms.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which the Company's headquarters is located, regardless of where Employee performs work.

BY SIGNING BELOW, EMPLOYEE ACKNOWLEDGES READING AND UNDERSTANDING THIS AGREEMENT IN ITS ENTIRETY.
